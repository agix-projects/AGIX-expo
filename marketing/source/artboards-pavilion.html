01 — The Mark

The canopy and the wheat. An amber canopy — the pavilion — shelters the AGIX wheat, with field rows beneath: a nation's agrifood story, hosted under one roof. The mark inherits the expo's wheat so every pavilion is unmistakably part of AGIX. Use the dark tile on screens and event signage; the light variant on print and white surfaces. Clear space: half the canopy's width on all sides. Never rotate, recolour or outline the mark.

06 — Usage Rules

Pair the mark with the endorsement ribbon on all public materials.
Category colour appears in the badge and accents only — grounds stay green.
Partner-country flags and names sit beside, never inside, the mark.
Minimum mark size: 32 px digital / 12 mm print.
Photography: real farms, markets and machinery — no generic stock gloss.
The wheat is shared with the master brand — never redraw or restyle it.
