# AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 — Social Media Automation Plan

**Purpose:** This document defines how social media for the AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 (Friday 9 – Saturday 10 April 2027, Amadeo Event Center, Ebeano Tunnel Road, Enugu, Nigeria; organised by AGIX Africa; www.agixafrica.com/expo2027) is run at ≥90% automation with ≤10% human involvement. It covers platform strategy, the content pipeline, community management with escalation rules, the performance loop, countdown campaign automation, and the recommended tooling stack with configuration instructions.

**Owner:** Virtual Marketing Assistant (autonomous) / **Human approval required for:** weekly batch-approval of scheduled content; all replies flagged as complaints, legal issues, political sensitivity, financial commitments or reputational risk; any post or reply naming unconfirmed partners, sponsors, speakers or statistics; granting and revoking account access.

## 2. Content pipeline workflow

**Stage 1 — Generation (autonomous).** Every Thursday the Assistant generates the following week's master posts from the content calendar (see /home/user/AGIX-expo/01-content/content-calendar-weeks-01-08.md), the evergreen bank and the performance loop's reuse queue. Each master post = core message + objective + CTA + link.

**Stage 2 — Reformatting per platform (autonomous).** Each master post is adapted using the caption adaptation rules below — never copy-pasted identically across platforms.

**Caption adaptation rules:**
- LinkedIn: professional framing, business outcome first, 60–150 words, 3–4 hashtags, no emoji-led lines.
- Facebook: warm, plain language, second person, 30–80 words, question or clear CTA, 2–3 hashtags.
- Instagram: hook line first, caption 20–60 words, 5–8 hashtags after the copy, "link in bio" for destinations.
- X: ≤260 characters, punchy, one link max, 2–3 hashtags.
- TikTok: conversational on-screen framing, caption ≤100 characters plus 3–4 hashtags, spoken CTA in the video.
- YouTube: descriptive title ≤70 characters, first 2 lines of description carry the CTA and link, chapters where relevant.
- WhatsApp: no hashtags, 2–4 sentences, one link, always ends with opt-out line ("Reply STOP to opt out.").

## 3. Community management

### 3.1 Monitoring cadence
- **Standard period (now to 28 days out):** all inboxes, comments and mentions checked 3×/day (09:00, 13:00, 17:00 WAT); response target 4 working hours.
- **Ramp-up (28 days out to event):** checks every 2 hours, 08:00–20:00; response target 2 hours.
- **Event week and event days:** continuous monitoring 07:00–21:00; response target 30 minutes.
- Brand-mention listening (name variants: "AGIX", "AGIX 2027", "AGIX Expo", "AGIX Africa") reviewed daily; negative-sentiment mentions routed through the triage matrix immediately.

### 3.2 Enquiry triage matrix

| Enquiry type | Action | Route |
|---|---|---|
| Dates, venue, directions, opening hours | Auto-reply | Template, immediate |
| General "what is AGIX?" questions | Auto-reply | Template, immediate |
| Registration process questions | Auto-reply | Template, immediate |
| Exhibitor enquiry (no pricing asked) | Auto-reply | Template + route to email sequence |
| Pricing questions (stands, sponsorship, tickets) | Draft-for-approval | Template drafted, human approves before send |
| Sponsorship enquiry | Draft-for-approval | Template drafted, human approves; logged for Partnerships |
| Speaking request | Draft-for-approval | Acknowledgement template; programme team decides |
| Media request | Draft-for-approval | Acknowledgement template; human approves |
| Partnership enquiry | Draft-for-approval | Template drafted, human approves |
| Complaint (any) | Escalate | Acknowledgement template may be sent; substantive reply human-only |
| Legal or contractual matters | Escalate | No automated reply beyond acknowledgement |
| Political or politically-adjacent comments | Escalate | No automated engagement |
| Requests implying financial commitment | Escalate | Human approval before any reply |
| Potential reputational risk (viral criticism, misinformation about the event) | Escalate | Human decision on response strategy |

### 3.4 Escalation rules (explicit)
The Assistant must **never** reply autonomously — beyond the neutral acknowledgement template — to anything involving:
- **Complaints** about the event, team, partners or communications;
- **Legal issues** (contracts, IP, threats of action, regulatory questions);
- **Political sensitivity** (party politics, government criticism, elections, communal issues);
- **Financial commitments** (agreeing prices, discounts, refunds, payment terms);
- **Reputational risk** (viral criticism, misinformation, safety allegations, anything about identifiable individuals).

Escalated items are logged with a screenshot/link, flagged to the human owner within 30 minutes during monitoring hours, and receive no further automated engagement until a human directs the response. Human approval is required **before** any reply is posted on these topics — including edits to acknowledgements.

## 4. Performance loop

**Weekly metrics to pull (every Monday, per platform):**
- LinkedIn: impressions, engagement rate, clicks, follower change, top post.
- Facebook: reach, engagement, link clicks, page follows, top post.
- Instagram: reach, saves, shares, profile visits, link-in-bio clicks, top post.
- X: impressions, engagement rate, link clicks, follower change, top post.
- TikTok: views, watch-through rate, followers, top video.
- YouTube: views, watch time, subscribers, top video.
- WhatsApp: list size, opt-outs, link clicks (via tracked links).
- Cross-channel: enquiry form submissions and registrations attributed by UTM.

**High-performer reuse rule:** any post whose engagement rate exceeds 2× its platform's trailing 4-week average is added to the reuse queue; it is refreshed (new visual or angle, same core message) and rescheduled no sooner than 3 weeks after the original. Any post below 0.25× average is flagged and its topic/format deprioritised for 4 weeks.

**Weekly performance summary template (auto-generated, sent to human owner every Monday):**

> 4. Bottom post (link, hypothesis — one line)
> 5. Funnel: link clicks → form submissions → registrations/enquiries (UTM-attributed)
> 6. Reuse queue additions and next week's planned experiments
> 7. Escalations/complaints this week: [count + status]

## 6. Tooling options and recommended stack

### 6.1 Options considered

| Tool | Covers | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Buffer | LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok, YouTube Shorts | Low-cost, simple approval-friendly queues; strong for a lean operation |
| Hootsuite | All major platforms + listening | More powerful inbox/listening; materially higher cost |
| Meta Business Suite | Facebook + Instagram (native) | Free scheduling, unified FB/IG inbox, basic automated responses |
| Native schedulers (X, TikTok, YouTube) | Their own platforms | Free; adequate where a paid tool isn't justified |
| WhatsApp Business app | WhatsApp broadcasts | Free; broadcast lists capped per list — segment lists; Channels for one-to-many |
| Metricool / Later (alternatives) | Multi-platform scheduling + reporting | Comparable low-cost alternatives to Buffer if pricing shifts |

4. **WhatsApp Business app (free)** — weekly broadcast; upgrade path to WhatsApp Business Platform via [BSP placeholder] if list growth demands.
