# AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 — Email Automation Sequences

**Purpose:** This document defines the segmented email automation programme for the AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 (Friday 9 – Saturday 10 April 2027, Amadeo Event Center, Ebeano Tunnel Road, Enugu, Nigeria; organised by AGIX Africa; www.agixafrica.com/expo2027). It contains full, ready-to-send copy for the three core acquisition sequences, structured sequences for all other segments, reusable countdown/logistics and post-event emails, and the global sending rules the automation must obey.

**Owner:** Virtual Marketing Assistant (autonomous) / **Human approval required for:** any email containing pricing or financial commitments; first send of each new sequence template; emails to government institutions, international partners and media; complaint responses; anything touching legal, political or reputational matters. All other sends run autonomously within the global rules below.

## 8. Global rules

### 8.1 Sending windows
- **Standard sends:** Tuesday–Thursday, 08:30–11:30 or 15:00–17:00 West Africa Time (WAT). Monday and Friday reserved for time-critical sends only.
- **Transactional emails** (confirmations, onboarding): sent immediately at any time.
- **No marketing sends** on Sundays or Nigerian public holidays ([public holiday calendar placeholder]).
- International partner sends adjusted to the recipient's local business hours where the timezone is known.

### 8.2 Frequency caps
- Maximum **2 marketing emails per contact per week**, across all sequences combined; transactional emails excluded.
- A contact active in multiple sequences receives only the **highest-priority** email in a collision (priority: deadline > confirmation follow-up > urgency > value > newsletter).
- Minimum **48 hours** between any two marketing emails to the same contact.
- Countdown/logistics emails override the weekly cap in the final 7 days before the event, capped at 1/day.

### 8.3 Opt-out language (actual footer copy — required on every marketing email)

> You are receiving this email because you registered interest in the AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027, subscribed to updates from AGIX Africa, or interacted with our team about the event.
>
> Prefer fewer emails? [Update your preferences]. Want none at all? [Unsubscribe] — one click, effective immediately, no questions asked.
>
> AGIX Africa · [postal address placeholder] · [contact email]
> AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 · 9–10 April 2027 · Amadeo Event Center, Ebeano Tunnel Road, Enugu, Nigeria · www.agixafrica.com/expo2027

### 8.4 Data protection (NDPR/GDPR-aware)
- All contact data is collected with a stated purpose and lawful basis, processed in line with the **Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR)** and, for contacts in applicable jurisdictions, the **GDPR**.
- Consent records (source, timestamp, purpose) are stored for every contact; purchased lists are **not** used.
- Unsubscribes are honoured immediately and globally across sequences; suppression lists are permanent unless the contact re-opts-in.
- Data subject requests (access, correction, deletion) are escalated to a human and completed within statutory timelines; the privacy notice is linked in every footer ([privacy policy link placeholder]).
- Personal data is never shared with sponsors or exhibitors without explicit, specific consent.

### 8.5 Deliverability practices
- Authenticate the sending domain with **SPF, DKIM and DMARC** before the first campaign send; use a dedicated subdomain (e.g. news.[domain placeholder]) for marketing volume.
- Warm up sending volume gradually; monitor bounce and complaint rates per send (target: hard bounces <2%, complaints <0.1%) and pause any sequence breaching them pending human review.
- Sunset policy: contacts with no opens/clicks in 120 days receive one re-engagement email, then move to a suppressed "dormant" list.
- Plain, honest subject lines; no misleading "RE:/FW:" prefixes; every email must render correctly text-only; list-unsubscribe headers enabled.
