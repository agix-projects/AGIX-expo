Visa Information

Visas for Travel to Nigeria

📋 Visa Requirements

  • Most foreign nationals require a Nigerian entry visa before travel
  • Business visitors from eligible countries may apply for Visa-on-Arrival (VoA)
  • Exhibitors, sponsors and delegates may request an official AGIX Invitation Letter
  • Passport must be valid for at least 6 months beyond arrival
  • Visa rules vary by nationality — check Nigeria Immigration guidelines

🗂️ Documents Needed

  • Valid passport with minimum 6-month validity
  • Completed visa application or VoA request form
  • Proof of return or onward travel booking
  • Hotel reservation or accommodation confirmation
  • Invitation letter from AGIX Africa (for business or expo-related travel)

🤝 How AGIX Supports You

  • Official invitation letters for confirmed exhibitors and sponsors
  • Assistance with Visa-on-Arrival documentation
  • Guidance on completing immigration and travel paperwork
  • Direct support via the AGIX Travel & Visa Desk
  • Updated visa guidelines, embassy contacts and processing timelines

Getting There

✈️ Air Travel

Akanu Ibiam International Airport (ENU), Enugu — typical connections:

  • Lagos (LOS)
  • Abuja (ABV)
  • Addis Ababa (ET)
  • Some regional flights possible

🚌 Ground Transportation

Interstate transport: parks & terminals, Enugu. Rideshare & taxis:

  • Airport taxis & ride-hailing services available
  • Partner hotels offer shuttle services
  • Approx. 15–20 minutes from the airport to the venue
  • AGIX will publish official transport partners
