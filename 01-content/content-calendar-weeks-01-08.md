# AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 — Content Calendar: Weeks 1–8 (13 July – 6 September 2026)

**Purpose:** This document is the working content calendar for the first eight weeks of the AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 marketing programme. It contains ready-to-publish draft copy for every planned post across all channels, together with the cadence, hashtag strategy, tone guide, approval workflow and an evergreen content bank. The event takes place Friday 9 – Saturday 10 April 2027 at the Amadeo Event Center, Ebeano Tunnel Road, Enugu, Nigeria, organised by AGIX Africa.

**Owner:** Virtual Marketing Assistant (autonomous) / **Human approval required for:** weekly batch-approval of drafted posts before scheduling; any post touching complaints, legal issues, political sensitivity, financial commitments, or reputational risk; any post naming a partner, sponsor, speaker or statistic not yet confirmed in writing.

---

## 2. Hashtag strategy

- **Anchor hashtags (every relevant post):** #AGIX2027 #Agribusiness #Agritech
- **Reach hashtags (rotate 1–2 per post):** #AfricaAgriculture #AgTechAfrica #FoodSecurity #AgriInvestment #NigeriaAgriculture #EnuguState #AgriInnovation #Agripreneur
- **Rules:** LinkedIn max 3–4 hashtags; Instagram 5–8 (place after copy); X max 2–3; Facebook 2–3; never hashtag WhatsApp or email. #AGIX2027 appears on every social post. No trending-topic hijacking without human approval.

## 3. Tone guide

- Professional, commercially persuasive, credible; British English throughout (organiser, programme, optimisation).
- Confident but never hyped: no invented statistics, no "biggest ever" claims, no unconfirmed names. Anything unconfirmed appears as a [square-bracket placeholder] and blocks publication until resolved.
- Warm and practical on Facebook/WhatsApp; authoritative and opportunity-led on LinkedIn/email; concise and energetic on X; visual-first on Instagram.
- Every post has exactly one CTA. Sentences short. Active voice.

## 4. Approval workflow

1. **Draft generation (autonomous):** the Virtual Marketing Assistant generates the coming week's drafts from this calendar every Thursday.
2. **Human batch-approval (weekly, ≤30 minutes):** drafts are compiled into a single approval sheet; the human reviewer approves, edits or rejects each row and confirms any placeholders resolved. Posts containing unresolved [placeholders] cannot be approved.
3. **Scheduling (autonomous):** approved posts are queued in the scheduling tool for their listed dates/times; publication status updated automatically.
4. **Exception path:** any content involving complaints, legal issues, political sensitivity, financial commitments or reputational risk is escalated for individual human sign-off and is excluded from batch approval.
5. **Performance logging (autonomous):** the "Performance result" column is populated 7 days after publication and feeds the weekly performance summary.
