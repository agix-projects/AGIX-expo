# Reporting Templates

**AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027**
Friday 9 – Saturday 10 April 2027 | Amadeo Event Center, Ebeano Tunnel Road, Enugu, Nigeria
Organiser: AGIX Africa | www.agixafrica.com/expo2027

**Purpose:** This document provides the standing reporting pack for the expo marketing programme: (a) the weekly marketing report template, (b) the monthly strategic report template, and (c) the daily workflow checklist with its daily output format. All templates are fill-in-ready; data sources are the lead tracker, KPI workbook and platform analytics defined in the companion operations documents.

**Owner:** Virtual Marketing Assistant (autonomous) / **Human approval required for:** none for producing reports; decisions flagged inside reports follow `approval-escalation-register.md`. Any external sharing of report contents requires approval (confidential information, A7).

## Part C — Daily workflow checklist and output format

### C1. Daily workflow checklist (executed each working morning, in order)

| # | Step | What it means in practice |
|---|------|---------------------------|
| 1 | Review event timeline | Check days-to-event, upcoming countdown milestones (9 January / 8 February / 10 March / 26 March / 2 April 2027) and any date-driven deliverables due within 14 days. |
| 2 | Review outstanding tasks | Sweep the task board and yesterday's carried-over items; nothing silently drops. |
| 3 | Review campaign performance | Scan yesterday's email, social and (when live) ad numbers for anomalies worth action today. |
| 4 | Review overdue follow-ups | Pull the tracker's overdue-actions and urgent-flag views (rules U1–U7); these outrank new work. |
| 5 | Review new opportunities | New inbound enquiries, replies, mentions and referral leads; log all into the tracker. |
| 6 | Prioritise the day | Order: urgent follow-ups → deadline-driven deliverables → pipeline progression → content production → improvements. |
| 7 | Produce materials | Draft the day's content, outreach and documents. |
| 8 | Execute approved actions | Send approved emails, publish approved batch content, run standing-approval work. |
| 9 | Record work | Update tracker rows, task board, KPI data entry; log all decisions taken. |
| 10 | Present approval items | Compile the daily approval digest per `approval-escalation-register.md` §2.2 and issue the daily output (C2). |

### C2. Daily output format

```
DAILY OUTPUT — AGIX Expo 2027 — [DD/MM/YYYY] — [n] days to event

ACTIONS COMPLETED
- [action — outcome]

ACTIONS SCHEDULED
- [action — when — what triggers it]

LEADS REQUIRING ATTENTION
- [Organisation — status — reason flagged — proposed action]

DRAFTS REQUIRING APPROVAL
- [item — category — link — needed by]

RISKS / ISSUES
- [risk or issue — severity — proposed mitigation]

CRITICAL NEXT ACTIONS
1. [most important action for tomorrow]
2. [second]
3. [third]
```

Conventions: the daily output is short (one screen); anything requiring a decision appears in DRAFTS REQUIRING APPROVAL or the digest, never buried in prose; an empty section is written as "None today" rather than omitted, so absence is deliberate and visible.
