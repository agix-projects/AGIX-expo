# Next 7 Days — Priority Task List (10–17 July 2026)

**AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027**
Friday 9 – Saturday 10 April 2027 | Amadeo Event Center, Ebeano Tunnel Road, Enugu, Nigeria
Organiser: AGIX Africa | www.agixafrica.com/expo2027 | Event is approximately 9 months away.

**Purpose:** This is the execution-level task list for the first week of the marketing programme, 10–17 July 2026. It sequences foundation work (facts, access, systems) ahead of production work (lists, drafts, content) so that nothing is published or sent before its dependencies are met.

**Owner:** Virtual Marketing Assistant (autonomous) / **Human approval required for:** confirming canonical facts, granting all credentials/access, approving the strategy pack, prospectus outlines and pricing placeholders, approving the press release and content batches, and registering accounts that require a human identity or payment method.

Effort key: S = under 1 hour, M = 1–3 hours, L = half day or more.

---

## Day 1 — Friday 10 July: facts and access (nothing else proceeds safely without these)

**1. Confirm canonical event facts and contact details with organiser.**
- Owner: Human (organiser confirms; Assistant prepares the one-page confirmation sheet). Effort: S (each side).
- Dependencies: none — first task of the programme.
- Definition of done: organiser has confirmed in writing: event name, dates (Friday 9 – Saturday 10 April 2027), venue (Amadeo Event Center, Ebeano Tunnel Road, Enugu), website URL, official enquiry email, official phone/WhatsApp number, and the named spokesperson. Confirmation stored in `07-approvals-archive`.

**2. Issue the consolidated access-request list to the organiser.**
- Owner: Assistant (prepare and send); Human (grant). Effort: S to prepare; M for human to action.
- Dependencies: none.
- Definition of done: a single written request covering exactly:
  - Official email account or sending alias for outreach (e.g. [expo@agixafrica.com — to confirm]) with credentials or delegated access.
  - Admin/editor access to existing social accounts (Facebook page, Instagram, LinkedIn page, X, YouTube — whichever exist), or confirmation none exist yet (see Task 12).
  - Website CMS/editor access for www.agixafrica.com/expo2027 (or webmaster contact) plus permission to install GA4 and embed forms; GA4 property access if one exists.
  - DNS access or webmaster contact for email-domain authentication records (SPF/DKIM) for the email tool.
  - Canva account access (or approval to create one on the official email).
  - Google Drive: creation of/access to the shared programme folder, and a shared Google Calendar.
  - Any existing contact lists or past enquiry records, with a note on how consent was obtained (see Task 11).

## Day 2 — Weekend/Monday 13 July: approvals in flight, systems stood up

**3. Present the strategy pack for approval.**
- Owner: Assistant (present); Human (approve). Effort: S to present; M for human review. SLA per approval register: 3 working days.
- Dependencies: Task 1 (facts confirmed).
- Definition of done: strategy pack (positioning, audiences, channel plan, timeline, KPI proposals from `kpi-dashboard.md`) delivered as digest items; organiser decision recorded in the approval register.

**4. Present exhibitor and sponsor prospectus outlines with proposed tier/pricing placeholders for approval.**
- Owner: Assistant (draft outlines); Human (approve structure and provide/approve pricing inputs). Effort: M.
- Dependencies: Task 1.
- Definition of done: both outlines (sections, package tiers such as [Platinum/Gold/Silver/Bronze — names pending], placeholder pricing clearly marked "[pending approval — not for external use]") delivered for decision; recorded in register. No prospectus leaves the building until pricing is approved.

**5. Stand up the lead tracker.**
- Owner: Assistant. Effort: M.
- Dependencies: Task 2 (Drive access) — can be built in scratch workspace immediately and moved once Drive is granted.
- Definition of done: Google Sheet created from `lead-tracker-template.csv`, example rows deleted, data-validation dropdowns and scoring formulas live, urgency-flag conditional formatting working, saved views from `lead-management-system.md` §7 configured, daily backup routine noted.

## Days 3–4 — Tuesday 14 – Wednesday 15 July: pipeline foundation

**6. Build the first 100-company exhibitor target list.**
- Owner: Assistant. Effort: L.
- Dependencies: Task 5 (tracker live). Not dependent on approvals — research only, no outreach yet.
- Definition of done: 100 organisations in the tracker across priority sectors (machinery, inputs, agritech, processing, livestock/aquaculture, irrigation, finance), each with sector, country, source noted, best-available public contact route, and fit score; contacts marked "[to verify]" where not confirmed; zero invented email addresses.

**7. Draft the launch press release for approval.**
- Owner: Assistant (draft); Human (approve — A-category: public statement). Effort: M.
- Dependencies: Tasks 1 and 3 (facts confirmed; strategy direction approved), spokesperson name (see decisions list).
- Definition of done: release drafted using only confirmed facts, quote attributed to [spokesperson — pending Task 1], no unverifiable claims, distribution list of media targets attached; submitted via daily digest.

**8. Set up the email marketing tool.**
- Owner: Assistant (configure); Human (create account on official email, approve domain-authentication DNS changes). Effort: M.
- Dependencies: Task 2 (email account, DNS/webmaster route).
- Definition of done: Brevo (or approved alternative per `martech-automation-architecture.md`) account live on the official email, sending domain authenticated (SPF/DKIM verified), master list and segments (Exhibitor/Sponsor/Delegate/Media/Partner) created, signature and footer with venue and dates configured, unsubscribe handling verified.

## Day 5 — Thursday 16 July: content and channels

**9. Prepare week-1 and week-2 social content batch for approval.**
- Owner: Assistant (produce); Human (approve batch — proposed as the first standing approval). Effort: L.
- Dependencies: Tasks 1 and 3; Canva access (Task 2) for visuals.
- Definition of done: 10–14 posts (announcement, save-the-date with countdown, venue/Enugu spotlight, why-exhibit, why-attend, sector spotlights) with copy, visual and channel per post, delivered as one approval item; publication only after approval and only to verified handles (Task 12).

**10. Import existing contacts into the email tool — with consent check.**
- Owner: Assistant (import and document); Human (confirm consent basis, provide the lists). Effort: M.
- Dependencies: Tasks 2 and 8.
- Definition of done: any existing lists reviewed for consent basis before import; contacts without a documentable consent basis are NOT imported to marketing lists (held separately for a permission-refresh campaign proposal); import deduplicated and tagged by source; consent note stored in `07-approvals-archive`.

## Days 6–7 — Friday 17 July: handles, wrap-up, first weekly report

**11. Register/verify social media handles for the expo.**
- Owner: Assistant (research availability, prepare names/bios); Human (create accounts where identity/phone verification is required, or grant admin on existing ones). Effort: M.
- Dependencies: Tasks 1 and 2.
- Definition of done: consistent handle set (proposed: [@agixexpo or aligned with existing AGIX Africa handles — pending approval]) secured or confirmed on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X and YouTube; bios carry confirmed facts (dates, venue, website); credentials stored per the organiser's preferred method; accounts connected to Meta Business Suite/Buffer.

**12. Close out week 1: first weekly report and week-2 plan.**
- Owner: Assistant. Effort: M.
- Dependencies: all above (reports on their status).
- Definition of done: first weekly marketing report issued per `reporting-templates.md` Part A, including status of every task on this list, all outstanding approvals restated, and week-2 priorities proposed.
