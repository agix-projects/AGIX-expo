# AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 — Marketing Operations System

**Event:** AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027
**Dates:** Friday 9 – Saturday 10 April 2027
**Venue:** Amadeo Event Center, Ebeano Tunnel Road, Enugu, Nigeria
**Organiser:** AGIX Africa
**Website:** www.agixafrica.com/expo2027

This repository is the operational home of the expo's marketing, communications and promotion programme. It is maintained by the Virtual Marketing Assistant, which acts as the operational owner of the programme — producing and executing campaign plans, calendars, copy, outreach systems, trackers and reports — with human involvement limited to approvals, credentials, key meetings and signatures (target operating model: at least 90% automated, no more than 10% human).

## How this system works

1. **Everything is execution-ready.** Documents contain actual copy (emails, scripts, posts, letters, press releases), not descriptions of copy. Items that cannot be verified or are pending a decision are marked with `[square-bracket placeholders]` — nothing unconfirmed is ever presented as fact.
2. **Autonomy with guardrails.** The Assistant proceeds autonomously on low-risk operational work (drafting, list-building, scheduling approved content, lead hygiene, reporting). Anything involving money, contracts, legal or political sensitivity, partner/speaker announcements, or reputational risk goes through the approval register before release. See `04-operations/approval-escalation-register.md`.
3. **A daily working cycle** (review timeline → tasks → performance → overdue follow-ups → opportunities → prioritise → produce → execute approved actions → record → present approvals) drives the operation. See `04-operations/reporting-templates.md`.
4. **No organisation is ever described as a confirmed partner, sponsor, speaker or exhibitor unless confirmation is documented.**

## What the organiser needs to do first

The Assistant's immediate blockers are listed in `04-operations/next-7-days-priority-list.md`. In summary:

1. Confirm canonical event facts and the public contact email/phone for campaigns.
2. Provide or approve access: email marketing account, social handles, website/analytics, Canva, shared drive, WhatsApp Business.
3. Approve the strategy pack, proposed targets (baseline/target/stretch), and exhibitor/sponsor tier structures and pricing.
4. Indicate a paid-advertising budget range — no spend is committed without explicit approval.
5. Name the official spokesperson for press materials.

## Brand-protection rules (apply to every output)

- Verified event information only; confirmed facts clearly separated from proposals.
- No exaggerated or unsupported claims; no invented statistics.
- No trademark symbol unless specifically instructed.
- Confidential commercial information stays out of public materials.
- Consistent messaging across channels (see `00-strategy/03-value-proposition-framework.md`).
- Data-protection and opt-out practices in all email and WhatsApp campaigns (NDPR/GDPR-aware).
