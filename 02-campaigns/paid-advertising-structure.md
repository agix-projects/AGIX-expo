## 7. Kill / Scale Rules

Applied at ad and ad-set/ad-group level after a minimum learning window ([7 days or 1,000 impressions per ad, whichever is later — to be approved]). The Assistant may **pause** losers autonomously; **any budget increase requires human approval**.

**Kill (pause) when any of:**
- Spend ≥ [3×] target CPL/CPR with zero conversions;
- CTR < [50%] of the campaign's proposed floor for 7 consecutive days;
- CPL/CPR > [2×] proposed target over a rolling 14 days despite creative rotation;
- Landing-page conversion from that ad < [50%] of campaign average (signals mis-matched promise);
- Any compliance concern (claim accuracy, unapproved copy) — pause immediately and escalate.

**Scale (propose to human) when all of:**
- CPL/CPR ≤ [75%] of proposed target over ≥ 14 days;
- Volume stable or growing at current budget (no fatigue signal: frequency < [3] on Meta, CTR trend flat/up);
- Downstream quality confirmed (e.g. enquiries becoming meetings/bookings, registrations from priority audience groups).
- Proposed step: +[20–30%] budget increments, one change per 72 hours, human-approved each step.

**Standing rules:** losers' budgets return to reserve, not automatically to winners; creative refresh every [3–4] weeks per campaign; no new audience, geography or platform without approval; all changes logged in the weekly report.
