# Exhibitor Recruitment Campaign
## AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027

**Purpose:** This document is the complete, execution-ready exhibitor recruitment system for the AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 (Friday 9 – Saturday 10 April 2027, Amadeo Event Center, Ebeano Tunnel Road, Enugu, Nigeria). It covers target identification, outreach copy across all channels, objection handling, lead scoring, conversion tracking and exhibitor onboarding.

**Owner:** Virtual Marketing Assistant (autonomous) / **Human approval required for:** pricing quotations, contractual commitments, discounts or concessions, final target-list sign-off before first outreach wave, and any deviation from the approved copy in this document.

## 4. Sector-Specific Message Angles

| Sector | Pain point | Message hook |
|---|---|---|
| Agricultural machinery | Reaching mechanisation buyers in Nigeria's South East is costly through field sales alone | Demonstrate equipment live to farmers, cooperatives and institutional buyers gathered in one venue over two days |
| Seeds and crop inputs | Counterfeit products and weak last-mile awareness undermine brand trust | Put your genuine product directly in front of farmers, agro-dealers and extension networks |
| Fertilisers | Distribution partnerships in emerging producing regions are hard to originate remotely | Meet distributors, blenders and large growers face-to-face in a dedicated agribusiness environment |
| Irrigation | Buyers hesitate without seeing systems working | A live-demo-friendly venue to show irrigation solutions to farm owners and project buyers |
| Precision agriculture | The value proposition needs demonstration, not brochures | Two days of hands-on demonstrations to progressive farmers, agribusinesses and investors |
| Agricultural drones | Regulation-aware buyers want to see capability and talk compliance | Showcase capability to farm enterprises, service buyers and government stakeholders in one place |
| Farm management software | Long sales cycles; users need to touch the product | Run live product tours for agribusiness decision-makers actively looking for solutions |
| Food processing | Sourcing reliable raw-material supply and offtake partners | Connect with producers, cooperatives and buyers along the full value chain |
| Packaging | Agri-processors underestimate packaging's margin impact | Present packaging innovation to processors and exporters seeking market-ready presentation |
| Cold-chain and storage | Post-harvest loss is a recognised problem but buyers rarely meet providers | Position your solution where producers, processors and financiers converge |
| Renewable energy for agriculture | Off-grid agri-energy buyers are fragmented and dispersed | Reach farm enterprises, processors and development programmes evaluating energy solutions |
| Livestock technology | Herd and poultry operators are hard to aggregate | Meet commercial livestock and poultry operators in a focused two-day setting |
| Aquaculture | Input and equipment suppliers struggle to reach fish farmers at scale | Exhibit to fish farmers, feed buyers and processors in a growing production region |
| Logistics | Agri-shippers choose partners on trust built face-to-face | Build relationships with exporters, processors and traders who move product every week |
| Agricultural finance | Loan books need qualified agri-SME pipeline | Originate qualified leads among agribusinesses actively investing in growth |
| Insurance | Agricultural insurance uptake requires education at point of interest | Educate and enrol farmers and agribusinesses in a high-attention environment |
| Research institutions | Research outputs rarely reach commercial adopters | Showcase innovations to the businesses positioned to commercialise them |
| Universities | Programmes and spin-outs need industry visibility | Present research, attract partners and connect students with employers |
| Agritech startups | Credibility and customer acquisition on a limited budget | An affordable stage to win customers, partners and investor attention |
| Development organisations | Programme visibility and private-sector linkage targets | Convene beneficiaries, partners and co-funders around your programme outcomes |
| Government agencies | Policy initiatives need private-sector engagement | Engage the agribusiness community directly on programmes and investment priorities |
| Export/trade organisations | Members need buyer and partner connections | Deliver a ready-made platform for member companies to meet the market |

## 9. Objection-Handling Table

| # | Objection | Response (spoken/written) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "We don't have budget for this." | "Completely understood — budgets are committed early. Two thoughts: first, stand options start at different levels, including compact packages [pricing — to be confirmed], so there may be an entry point that fits. Second, if 2026 budget is closed, the event is in April 2027 — this could sit in your 2027 allocation. Would it help if I sent the options so you can see what the real numbers look like before ruling it out?" |
| 2 | "The timing doesn't work for us." | "May I ask what makes April difficult? … If it's a product-cycle issue, April sits ahead of the main planting-season purchasing decisions in much of Nigeria, which is exactly why we chose it. If it's a staffing issue, many exhibitors run a stand with two people, and we can connect you with local staffing support [to be confirmed]. What would need to be true for the timing to work?" |
| 3 | "I've never heard of AGIX." | "That's fair — AGIX Africa is the organiser, and this Expo is a deliberate, professionally built platform rather than a legacy event. Everything is documented: the venue is contracted, the dates are fixed for 9–10 April 2027, and the visitor recruitment plan is already in motion across farmer networks, associations, media and digital channels. I'd rather show you than tell you — may I send the prospectus and walk you through exactly how visitors are being recruited?" |
| 4 | "It's a first edition — too risky." | "A first edition is a legitimate question, and here's the honest answer: what you trade in track record, you gain in position. First-edition exhibitors get premium stand locations, stronger share of voice, and founding-exhibitor visibility that later entrants can't buy. We are also transparent about our visitor recruitment plan and will share progress updates before the event, so you're not deciding blind. And your competitors face the same question — the ones who say yes own the floor." |
| 5 | "Enugu is far / logistics are difficult." | "Enugu is served by Akanu Ibiam International Airport and sits on major road corridors into the South East. The Amadeo Event Center is on Ebeano Tunnel Road within the city. We are preparing an exhibitor logistics guide covering freight, hotels and local services [to be confirmed], and can recommend handling partners. More importantly — the distance is exactly why exhibiting works: the buyers in this region are hard to reach any other way." |
| 6 | "I doubt the ROI." | "Then let's define it rather than debate it. What would make this pay for you — a number of qualified leads, one distributor signed, a certain volume of demos? … If we agree on that measure, I can show you how the format supports it: two full days, a targeted visitor campaign, demonstration space, and pre-event promotion of exhibitors on our channels. And if after seeing the prospectus you still can't see a path to that number, walking away is the right decision. Fair?" |
| 7 | "Just send me the information." | "Happily — I'll send the prospectus today. So I send the right sections: is your main interest leads, brand presence or partner recruitment? … Perfect. And rather than me chasing you, let's fix a ten-minute call for [day/time] once you've had a look — if it's a no at that point, tell me and I'll close the file. Does [day/time] work?" |
| 8 | "We already exhibit at another event." | "Good — that tells me exhibitions already work for you, which is most of the decision made. The question is coverage, not either/or: AGIX Expo 2027 gives you the South East Nigerian market and an audience assembled specifically around agribusiness and agritech. Many companies run more than one event precisely because each covers different buyers. May I show you how the audiences differ?" |
| 9 | "We tried exhibitions before and got nothing." | "Sorry to hear that — usually that comes down to audience fit or follow-up, not the format. Two things we do differently: the visitor campaign is targeted at named audience groups who buy in your category, and we support exhibitors with pre-event promotion and lead-capture guidance. If you tell me what went wrong last time, I'll tell you honestly whether we solve it." |
| 10 | "Decision is above my level." | "Understood — who would make the final call? … Would it help if I prepared a one-page internal summary you can put in front of [decision-maker], covering costs, audience and expected outcomes? I can also join a short call with them if that's easier. My job is to make you look good for bringing this to the table." |

## 12. Conversion Stages

1. **Identified** — on approved target list, profile complete.
2. **Contacted** — first outreach sent.
3. **Engaged** — opened/clicked/connected, no reply yet.
4. **In conversation** — replied or call completed.
5. **Qualified** — need, authority and interest confirmed; prospectus reviewed.
6. **Proposal/hold** — stand option held; pricing discussion (human-approved) under way.
7. **Committed** — booking form signed / written confirmation received.
8. **Paid** — invoice settled [payment terms — to be confirmed].
9. **Onboarded** — profile, logo and materials received; stand allocated.
10. **Lost/Deferred** — reason coded (budget, timing, fit, no response, competitor, other).
