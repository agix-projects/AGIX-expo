# Delegate Registration Campaign
## AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027

**Purpose:** This document is the complete, execution-ready delegate and visitor registration campaign for the AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 (Friday 9 – Saturday 10 April 2027, Amadeo Event Center, Ebeano Tunnel Road, Enugu, Nigeria). It defines audiences, the registration funnel, campaign waves and channels, the referral programme, and full promotional copy across every channel.

**Owner:** Virtual Marketing Assistant (autonomous) / **Human approval required for:** registration platform selection and any paid tooling, ticket pricing (if any paid categories are introduced), referral/ambassador incentives, radio buying and any paid promotion, bulk SMS/WhatsApp sends to purchased or partner lists, and final targets sign-off.

## 1. Audience Groups

| Group | Who they are | Primary motivation to attend |
|---|---|---|
| Farmers and cooperatives | Smallholder and commercial farmers, cooperative societies and unions | See equipment and inputs, learn techniques, access finance and buyers |
| Agribusiness owners | SME processors, aggregators, agro-dealers, service providers | Source suppliers, meet buyers, discover technology, network |
| Corporates | Food companies, input manufacturers, retailers and their teams | Supplier discovery, market intelligence, partnerships |
| Investors | Angel investors, funds, family offices, agri-lenders | Deal flow, sector insight, portfolio support |
| Policymakers | Federal, state and local government officials and legislators | Engage the value chain, showcase programmes, gather evidence |
| Researchers | Institutes and independent researchers | Industry linkage, dissemination, collaboration |
| Students | Agriculture, engineering, business and technology students | Careers, entrepreneurship exposure, learning |
| Development organisations | NGOs, donor programmes, implementing partners | Partner scouting, beneficiary linkage, visibility |
| International delegations | Trade missions, foreign agribusinesses, diaspora investors | Market entry, sourcing, partnership |

## 10. Reminder and No-Show Reduction Sequence

| Timing | Channel | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Instant | Email + SMS | Confirmation, QR pass, add-to-calendar, referral link |
| 30 days out (~10 March 2027) | Email | Programme highlights, exhibitor preview, travel planning nudge |
| 14 days out | Email | "Plan your visit" — venue map, session picks by audience group, group invite reminder |
| 7 days out | Email + SMS | Logistics: directions, parking, transport, entry process, pass reminder |
| 3 days out | WhatsApp/Email | Short personal-tone reminder + top 5 things not to miss |
| 1 day before | SMS + Email | "Tomorrow" message, doors-open time [tbc], pass ready check |
| Day 1 morning | SMS | Welcome + entry tips (arrive early, badge collection points) |
| Day 1 evening | SMS/Email | Day-2 programme highlights to registered non-attendees and day-1 visitors |
| Post-event | Email | Thank-you, highlights, feedback survey, 2028 interest capture |

**No-show reduction principles:** confirmations restate value not just logistics; every reminder includes one new reason to attend; commitment devices (session bookmarking, "invite a colleague"); transport/practicality friction addressed early; day-of messaging creates event-in-progress momentum.
