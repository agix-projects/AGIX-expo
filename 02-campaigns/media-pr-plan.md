# Media and Public Relations Plan
## AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027

**Purpose:** This document is the complete media and public-relations plan for the AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 (Friday 9 – Saturday 10 April 2027, Amadeo Event Center, Ebeano Tunnel Road, Enugu, Nigeria). It covers objectives, the media database, the press-release calendar with the full launch release, pitching and briefing materials, spokesperson preparation, media partnerships, accreditation, the press conference, event-day media operations and post-event reporting.

**Owner:** Virtual Marketing Assistant (autonomous) / **Human approval required for:** every press release before distribution, all spokesperson designations and quotes, media partnership agreements, accreditation approvals for sensitive cases, press-conference confirmation and any statement issued in the name of AGIX Africa.

---

## 1. Objectives

1. Establish the AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 as a credible, professionally organised platform in Nigerian and regional agribusiness media by [end of 2026].
2. Generate sustained earned-media coverage across the campaign phases (launch, build-up, countdown, event, post-event).
3. Drive measurable referral traffic and registrations from media coverage (tracked via UTM links supplied to media).
4. Support exhibitor and sponsorship sales with third-party credibility (coverage clippings used in sales materials).
5. Build a reusable media-relations asset (database, relationships, materials) for future editions.
6. Protect the organisation: no unverified claims, no unapproved statements, disciplined placeholder use until facts are confirmed.

## 7. Spokesperson Talking Points

Ten core points (for the designated spokesperson [name/title — to be confirmed]; final wording subject to human approval):

1. **What it is:** "The AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 is a two-day exhibition and conference bringing the whole agricultural value chain together — 9 and 10 April 2027 at the Amadeo Event Center in Enugu."
2. **Why it exists:** "Agriculture is being transformed by technology and investment, but the people driving that change rarely meet in one place. This Expo is that place."
3. **Why Enugu:** "The South East is an important and under-served agricultural economy. Enugu gives the sector a platform in its own region — accessible, practical and long overdue."
4. **Who it is for:** "Farmers and cooperatives, agribusiness owners, processors, investors, policymakers, researchers, students and international partners — there is a track for each of them."
5. **What visitors will see:** "Machinery, inputs, irrigation, drones, software, processing, cold-chain, finance — exhibited and demonstrated live, not just talked about."
6. **The organiser:** "AGIX Africa is building this as a professionally run, transparent platform — what we announce is what is confirmed, and nothing else."
7. **On being a first edition:** "Every important event had a first edition. We are earning trust the only way it can be earned — by being transparent about what we are building and then delivering it."
8. **The commercial case:** "Two days at the Expo replaces weeks of travel — for a farmer comparing equipment, an agribusiness finding buyers, or an investor meeting the sector."
9. **Partnerships:** "We are in conversations with organisations across finance, technology, media and development — and we will announce partners when, and only when, they are confirmed."
10. **Call to action:** "Registration is open now at www.agixafrica.com/expo2027 — visitors, exhibitors and partners can all start there."

## 9. Press Accreditation Process

**Form fields:**
- Full name; outlet/organisation; role (reporter, editor, photographer, camera operator, producer, freelancer, content creator);
- Media type (print/online/TV/radio/podcast/wire/influencer);
- Editor/commissioning contact for verification (name, email, phone);
- Press card or commissioning letter upload;
- Samples of recent relevant work (up to 3 links);
- Days attending (9 April / 10 April / both); equipment being brought (camera/drone [drone use subject to venue and regulatory clearance — to be confirmed]);
- Interview requests (who/topic — optional);
- Email, phone/WhatsApp; consent to media-communications contact.

**Approval workflow:**
1. Application received → auto-acknowledgement email within minutes.
2. Verification: outlet legitimacy, work samples, editor confirmation for freelancers.
3. Decision: standard cases approved by the Assistant against written criteria [criteria — to be approved]; borderline/sensitive cases (unverifiable outlets, influencers below threshold, previous conduct concerns) escalated to human decision.
4. Approval email with media badge details, media-centre information and embargo/house rules; rejections receive a courteous explanation and an alternative (attend as general visitor).
5. Accredited list maintained in the media tracker; badges printed for collection at the media desk.
6. Applications close [date — proposed: 5 April 2027]; late/on-site accreditation at organiser discretion.

9. **Lessons and recommendations** for the next edition.

## 13. Escalation Rules

1. **All press releases require human approval before distribution — no exceptions.**
2. All quotes attributed to any person require that person's written approval and human sign-off.
3. Crisis or negative-coverage situations (misreporting, incident at venue, partner dispute): the Assistant drafts a holding line but issues nothing; immediate human escalation.
4. No partner, sponsor or speaker is named publicly before contract/written confirmation and approval by both parties.
5. No statistics, projections or attendance claims are ever issued unless verified and human-approved.
6. Media enquiries requesting comment beyond the approved fact sheet and talking points are logged and escalated within [4] working hours.
