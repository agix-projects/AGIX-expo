AGIX Expo 2027

A Strategic Marketplace for Africa's Agricultural Transformation

AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 brings together agrifood companies, processors, innovators, investors, financiers, development partners and government institutions to accelerate Africa's agricultural transformation.

The Expo serves as a strategic marketplace for showcasing technologies, forming partnerships and unlocking investment opportunities across crops, livestock, aquaculture, processing, logistics and digital agriculture.
