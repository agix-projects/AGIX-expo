FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

When and where is the expo taking place?

The AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 runs from Friday 9 to Saturday 10 April 2027 at the Amadeo Event Centre, Ebeano Tunnel Rd, Achara – Enugu, Nigeria.

Is registration free?

Yes — visitor registration is free. Exhibitor stands and sponsorship packages are paid; select "Exhibitor" or "Sponsor / Partner" in the form and our team will contact you with a floor plan and rates.

How do I become an exhibitor or sponsor?

Register using the form above and choose "Exhibitor" or "Sponsor / Partner" as your category, or email hello@agixafrica.com / call +234 702 642 1626 to speak with the commercial team.

Can I apply to speak at the conference?

Yes — agribusiness leaders, agritech innovators, policymakers and experts are invited to apply. Select "Speaker" in the registration form and include your proposed topic in the message field.

How do I get to the venue?

The Amadeo Event Centre is on Ebeano Tunnel Rd in Achara, Enugu — easily reached from Akanu Ibiam International Airport and well connected to hotels and major roads across South-East Nigeria.
