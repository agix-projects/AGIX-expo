About the Expo

Where African Agriculture Meets Global Investment

The AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 is a continental marketplace for innovation, investment and value-chain growth in African agriculture. Organised by Africa Growth Industries Exchange (AGIX Africa) Ltd, the expo brings together agribusiness leaders, commercial farmers, agritech innovators, commodity traders, policymakers, financiers and development partners for high-level engagement.

The event serves as a platform for partnerships, market entry, capacity development and large-scale business visibility for companies operating along Africa's food and agritech value chains — from farm inputs and mechanisation to processing, logistics and export.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

When and where is the expo taking place?

The AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 runs from Thursday 28 to Saturday 30 January 2027 at the International Conference Centre (ICC), Enugu, Nigeria.

Is registration free?

Yes — visitor registration is free. Exhibitor stands and sponsorship packages are paid; select "Exhibitor" in the form and our team will contact you with a floor plan and rates.

How do I become an exhibitor or sponsor?

Register using the form above and choose "Exhibitor" as your category, or email hello@agixafrica.com / call +234 702 642 1626 to speak with the commercial team.

Can I apply to speak at the conference?

Yes — agribusiness leaders, agritech innovators, policymakers and experts are invited to apply. Select "Speaker" in the registration form and include your proposed topic in the message field.

How do I get to the venue?

The ICC Enugu is minutes from Akanu Ibiam International Airport and is well connected to hotels and major roads across South-East Nigeria.
