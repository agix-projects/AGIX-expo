# 90-Day Action Plan
## AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 — 10 July to 8 October 2026

**Purpose:** Week-by-week execution plan for the first 90 days of the campaign, covering the end of Phase 1 (Foundation & Market Positioning), all early Phase 2 (Awareness & Partnership Development) and the opening of Phase 3 (Exhibitor & Sponsor Acquisition). Every action is written at execution level; the assistant works through them autonomously except where an approval is flagged.

**Owner:** Virtual Marketing Assistant (autonomous) / **Human approval required for:** strategy and pricing sign-off, all public announcements and press materials, institutional/government letters, agreements and signatures, credentials and paid subscriptions, budget commitments.

**Operating rhythm throughout:** weekly automated performance report every Friday; approval queue batched and submitted every Monday; exceptions escalated immediately.

---

## Weeks 1–3 · Phase 1 · Foundation (10 – 30 July 2026)

### Week 1 (10–16 July) — Strategy lock and infrastructure start
**Focus:** get the strategy approved and the machine assembled.
1. Submit strategy suite (docs 01–05) for human approval; log requested changes.
2. Audit existing AGIX Africa assets: domain, website, social handles, email domain, brand files; produce gap list.
3. Select and configure the CRM [tool choice to be confirmed]; create pipelines for exhibitors, sponsors, partners, media.
4. Configure email marketing platform, sending domain authentication (SPF/DKIM) and sign-up form infrastructure.
5. Draft landing page wireframe and copy for www.agixafrica.com/expo2027 (hero, value proposition, interest-capture forms for exhibitor/sponsor/delegate/media).
6. Begin comparable-event landscape research (method per doc 01 §6); start the benchmark tracker.

**Deliverables:** approved strategy (or change log); asset audit; CRM configured; landing page draft. **Approvals:** strategy suite; tool subscriptions and credentials. **Success measures:** strategy approved; CRM and email platform live; landing page draft submitted.

### Week 2 (17–23 July) — Brand assets and target-list engine
**Focus:** brand kit and the first wave of list building.
1. Produce brand asset drafts: logo lockups for expo, colour/typography usage, social templates, email header, presentation template; submit for approval.
2. Build exhibitor target list v1: 200 companies across priority sectors (machinery, seeds/inputs, ag finance, agritech/software, processing) with contact roles, sources logged.
3. Build sponsor target list v1: 60 organisations across banks, telecoms, DFIs, agri corporates, insurers.
4. Build institutional list: relevant federal/state ministries, agencies, 30+ associations/cooperatives, embassies/trade missions with commercial sections.
5. Draft outreach template library v1: exhibitor sequence (4 touches), sponsor sequence (3 touches), association partnership letter, institutional briefing letter; submit for approval.
6. Continue landscape research; produce date-clash map around 9–10 April 2027.

**Deliverables:** brand kit draft; target lists (exhibitor 200 / sponsor 60 / institutional); template library v1. **Approvals:** brand assets; template library; institutional letter drafts. **Success measures:** lists complete with verified contact data ≥80%; templates approved or in revision.

### Week 3 (24–30 July) — Landing page live (soft) and content foundations
**Focus:** owned channels ready for launch.
1. Finalise landing page and publish in soft-launch mode (no promotion) with interest-capture forms wired to CRM; test all forms end-to-end.
2. Set up social scheduling tool; create profiles/refresh existing handles (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube) with approved branding.
3. Draft the August content calendar: launch week posts, positioning content, 12 evergreen posts across the five message pillars.
4. Draft launch press release no. 1 and media pitch note; build media contact list v1 (agri trade, business, Enugu-region outlets — 50 contacts).
5. Draft the exhibitor prospectus skeleton: event overview, audiences targeted, stand options structure, [pricing to be confirmed] placeholders, founding-exhibitor framing.
6. Prepare weekly reporting dashboard (list growth, social, pipeline, approvals outstanding).

**Deliverables:** landing page live (soft); social profiles ready; August content calendar; press release draft; prospectus skeleton. **Approvals:** landing page final copy; press release; content calendar; prospectus direction. **Success measures:** forms tested and routing to CRM; all launch assets in approval queue by Friday.

## Weeks 4–8 · Phase 2 · Awareness & Partnership Development (31 July – 3 September 2026)

### Week 4 (31 July – 6 August) — Public launch
**Focus:** announce the expo to the market.
1. Execute launch: publish announcement across all social channels, landing page promoted, launch email to any existing AGIX contacts [list access to be confirmed].
2. Distribute approved press release no. 1 to media list; log pickups; offer spokesperson interviews [spokesperson to be confirmed].
3. Launch LinkedIn organic cadence 3×/week and Meta cadence 3×/week from the approved calendar.
4. Send approved institutional briefing letters (tranche 1: ministries and top-10 associations).
5. Open interest-capture campaign: "Register your interest" pushed via social pinned posts and bios.
6. Monitor launch performance daily; produce launch-week report.

**Deliverables:** launch executed; press distribution done; institutional tranche 1 sent. **Approvals:** final launch assets; each institutional letter; interview participation. **Success measures:** launch live on all channels; first interest sign-ups recorded; ≥1 media pickup [proposed target pending approval].

### Week 5 (7–13 August) — Association and partner outreach
**Focus:** open trusted-channel partnerships.
1. Send association partnership outreach (tranche 2: 20 further associations/cooperatives) proposing member-benefit collaboration [terms to be confirmed].
2. Send embassy/trade-mission briefing notes (tranche 1: 10 missions with strongest agri-trade relevance).
3. Publish first thought-leadership post (South East agribusiness opportunity theme) on LinkedIn and blog.
4. Complete exhibitor prospectus full draft including stand packages structure and scenario pricing options for human decision.
5. Begin sponsor materials: sponsorship prospectus outline with tier structure options [pricing to be confirmed].
6. Start weekly interest-list nurture email (bi-weekly cadence to start).

**Deliverables:** partnership outreach tranches sent; prospectus full draft; nurture email live. **Approvals:** association terms framing; embassy notes; prospectus draft review; nurture email template. **Success measures:** association responses/meetings requested; interest list growing week on week.

### Week 6 (14–20 August) — Media relationships and content depth
**Focus:** convert launch attention into standing relationships.
1. Follow up all media contacts individually; offer story angles (regional economy, agritech, event preview); log relationship status in CRM.
2. Pitch two op-ed/feature concepts to trade and business outlets [author/spokesperson to be confirmed].
3. Produce and schedule content batch 2: pillar-based posts plus first short-form video script [production approach to be confirmed].
4. Expand exhibitor target list to 300 with tranche-2 sectors (irrigation, cold chain, packaging, livestock/aquaculture tech, renewables).
5. Research and document 3 candidate registration platform options with costs for human decision (needed live by early November per roadmap).
6. Prepare exhibitor outreach sequencing plan: send-order by sector, personalisation fields, follow-up timing.

**Deliverables:** media relationship log; content batch 2; platform options paper. **Approvals:** op-ed concepts; video approach; platform shortlist direction. **Success measures:** media relationships opened (logged, two-way) growing; list building on schedule.

### Week 7 (21–27 August) — Sponsor groundwork
**Focus:** prepare the sponsor engine before September outreach.
1. Finalise sponsorship prospectus draft: tiers, benefit schedules, category-exclusivity options, campaign-length visibility benefits; submit with pricing scenarios for approval.
2. Enrich sponsor target list: identify named decision-makers (marketing directors, corporate affairs, agri-desk heads) for top 30 accounts.
3. Draft sponsor outreach sequence and discovery-call framework; submit for approval.
4. Publish content batch: "why the South East, why now" positioning series.
5. Send association follow-ups; convert interested associations to concrete partnership proposals [terms to be approved per deal].
6. Landscape research checkpoint: publish internal benchmark summary (pricing norms observed, date clashes, differentiation confirmation).

**Deliverables:** sponsorship prospectus draft; enriched sponsor list; benchmark summary. **Approvals:** sponsorship structure and pricing scenarios; sponsor templates; each association proposal. **Success measures:** sponsor materials approval-ready; ≥1 association partnership in active negotiation [proposed target pending approval].

### Week 8 (28 August – 3 September) — Pre-sales readiness gate
**Focus:** everything ready for Phase 3 outbound.
1. Finalise exhibitor prospectus for sign-off — the critical-path approval for all exhibitor revenue.
2. Load exhibitor outreach sequences into CRM with personalisation tested; dry-run on internal addresses.
3. Build sales collateral: one-page exhibitor summary, objection-handling sheet (from doc 03), FAQ page on landing page.
4. Segment interest list captured to date; route exhibitor/sponsor interests into sales pipelines for priority contact.
5. Publish press release no. 2 angle options (e.g. "exhibitor sales open") for approval timing decision.
6. Produce end-of-Phase-2 report: audience growth, media relationships, partnership status vs proposed targets.

**Deliverables:** sign-off-ready prospectus; loaded sequences; sales collateral; phase report. **Approvals:** **exhibitor prospectus + pricing (critical path)**; press release no. 2; collateral. **Success measures:** readiness checklist 100%; approval obtained or escalated with impact note.

## Weeks 9–13 · Phase 3 opens · Exhibitor & Sponsor Acquisition (4 September – 8 October 2026)

### Week 9 (4–10 September) — Exhibitor sales launch
**Focus:** open outbound at full tempo.
1. Publish approved prospectus; announce "exhibitor applications open" across owned channels; distribute press release no. 2.
2. Launch outbound exhibitor sequence wave 1: 100 priority-sector companies (machinery, inputs, ag finance, agritech).
3. Route and respond to inbound prospectus requests within 24 hours (automated first response, personalised follow-up).
4. Launch sponsor outreach wave 1: top 15 accounts (banks, telecoms, DFIs) with approved sequence; propose discovery calls for human lead.
5. Begin weekly pipeline report (leads, conversations, proposals, commitments) against baseline/target/stretch.
6. Start retargeting audience pools (site visitors, video viewers) ready for October paid launch [budget approval pending].

**Deliverables:** sales launch executed; wave-1 sequences running; pipeline reporting live. **Approvals:** press release timing; discovery-call scheduling (human calendar); any deviation from approved templates. **Success measures:** wave-1 delivery/response rates; first exhibitor leads in pipeline; first sponsor calls booked.

### Week 10 (11–17 September) — Follow-up discipline and wave 2
**Focus:** work the pipeline, widen the top of funnel.
1. Execute follow-up touches for wave 1 (calls/emails per sequence); update CRM stages daily.
2. Launch exhibitor wave 2: next 100 companies (processing, packaging, cold chain, irrigation).
3. Prepare human-led sponsor discovery calls: briefing notes per account, proposal shells ready.
4. Publish exhibitor-facing content: "what your stand includes", floor plan concept teaser [floor plan to be confirmed with operations].
5. Send embassy/trade-mission tranche 2 (10 further missions) introducing exhibitor and delegation opportunities.
6. Test and refine sequences based on wave-1 response data; log learnings.

**Deliverables:** wave 2 live; sponsor call briefings; sequence optimisation note. **Approvals:** floor plan teaser; embassy tranche 2; sequence changes beyond approved templates. **Success measures:** follow-up completion 100% on schedule; response rate stable or improving.

### Week 11 (18–24 September) — Sponsor proposals and international push
**Focus:** convert sponsor interest into proposals; open international lanes.
1. Draft tailored sponsor proposals for accounts that completed discovery calls; submit each for approval before issue.
2. Launch exhibitor wave 3: 100 companies (livestock/aquaculture tech, renewables, logistics, insurance, research/universities, startups).
3. Publish startup zone concept and pre-application interest form [package pricing to be confirmed].
4. Begin international exhibitor outreach: 50 targets in Europe/Middle East/Asia sourced from trade-body directories and source-event exhibitor lists.
5. Draft media partnership proposal template for trade and broadcast outlets; submit for approval.
6. Mid-phase pipeline review: velocity check against 90-day proposed targets; corrective actions listed.

**Deliverables:** sponsor proposals issued (post-approval); waves 1–3 all live; international outreach started. **Approvals:** every sponsor proposal; startup package framing; media partnership template. **Success measures:** proposals issued; international responses logged; pipeline coverage vs target reviewed.

### Week 12 (25 September – 1 October) — Early-bird design and paid readiness
**Focus:** prepare October conversion levers.
1. Design founding-exhibitor early-bird offer (discount/benefit options with revenue impact scenarios); submit for approval.
2. Prepare October paid campaign plan: retargeting + LinkedIn lead-gen for exhibitors; audiences, creative, budget scenarios for approval.
3. Produce sponsor pipeline dossier for human review: account status, next actions, meeting requests.
4. Refresh content calendar for October: countdown-to-early-bird, exhibitor testimonials replaced by "why we're building this" founder-voice content (no invented testimonials).
5. Chase all open association and embassy threads; convert warm ones to concrete next steps.
6. Data hygiene sprint: dedupe CRM, verify bounce lists, refresh contact data on non-responders.

**Deliverables:** early-bird proposal; paid plan; sponsor dossier; October calendar. **Approvals:** early-bird terms; paid budget; October calendar. **Success measures:** October levers fully approved or escalated; CRM data quality checks passed.

### Week 13 (2–8 October) — 90-day review and Phase 3 acceleration setup
**Focus:** close the 90-day window with a full accounting and a loaded next quarter.
1. Produce the 90-day results report: every metric below vs baseline/target/stretch, with variance analysis and lessons.
2. Launch approved early-bird campaign to entire exhibitor pipeline and interest list.
3. Launch approved paid campaigns; set daily optimisation rules and weekly reporting.
4. Issue second round of sponsor follow-ups; schedule human meetings for October.
5. Confirm registration platform decision is on track for early-November go-live (per roadmap dependency); escalate if not.
6. Submit Q4 (Oct–Dec) detailed action plan for approval, rolling this format forward.

**Deliverables:** 90-day report; early-bird live; paid live; Q4 plan. **Approvals:** 90-day report conclusions; Q4 plan; platform decision. **Success measures:** report delivered on time; all October engines running.
