# Value Proposition Framework
## AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027

**Purpose:** This framework fixes what we promise each audience of the AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027, how we substantiate it without unverified claims, how we answer objections, and how messages cascade from master narrative to channel copy. All campaign copy must trace back to this document.

**Owner:** Virtual Marketing Assistant (autonomous) / **Human approval required for:** changes to the master proposition or boilerplate, any new public claim, competitive comparisons, statements referencing government or named organisations.

---

## 1. Master Proposition

> **The AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 brings the people who grow, process, finance, regulate and modernise African agriculture together for two days in Enugu — one venue, structured to help you sell, buy, invest and partner.**
> Friday 9 – Saturday 10 April 2027 · Amadeo Event Center, Ebeano Tunnel Road, Enugu, Nigeria · www.agixafrica.com/expo2027

**Universal proof approach (applies to every audience proposition below):** because this edition has no past-event statistics to cite, we substantiate with (a) verifiable facts — dates, venue, programme structure, confirmed features as they are approved; (b) transparent proposed targets labelled as targets, never as facts; (c) named participation only after written confirmation and approval; (d) market context drawn from citable public sources, always attributed; (e) design proof — showing the mechanics (matchmaking format, demo areas, delegation programme) that make outcomes plausible. We never invent attendance figures, past results, partners or endorsements.

## 2. Audience Propositions

### 2.1 Exhibitor

**Headline proposition:** Two days of direct access to the buyers, distributors and partners of South East Nigeria's agribusiness market — a region national events do not reach.

**Supporting points**
- Concentrated regional audience: farmers, cooperatives, SMEs, corporates and institutions targeted across the South East plus Lagos and Abuja.
- Founding-edition advantage: early exhibitors help shape the event and secure prime positions and visibility.
- Structured selling: stand packages plus [matchmaking programme to be confirmed] and demo opportunities, not passive footfall.
- Cost-efficient market entry versus building a regional sales tour independently.

**Proof approach:** publish the audience-acquisition plan and proposed visitor targets (baseline/target/stretch, labelled pending approval); share campaign progress updates (registrations to date) once real; showcase venue and programme facts.

**Objections and responses**

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "First edition — no track record." | Correct, and we will not pretend otherwise. You get founding-exhibitor pricing and positioning [terms to be confirmed], full transparency on registration progress, and a market no established event serves. |
| "Will the right buyers attend?" | Our acquisition plan targets buyers by name through associations, cooperatives and direct campaigns; we will share registration composition ahead of the event. |
| "Enugu is not our usual market." | That is the opportunity: an under-served commercial region with lower cost of entry than saturated Lagos/Abuja calendars. |
| "Too expensive for us." | Package tiers scale down to SME and startup levels [pricing to be confirmed]; compare against the cost of reaching this audience any other way. |

**Do:** sell access and structure; quote only approved facts; use "proposed target" language. **Don't:** promise attendance numbers, ROI figures, or name other exhibitors without written confirmation.

### 2.2 Sponsor

**Headline proposition:** Category leadership in front of the decision-makers of a fast-growing regional agribusiness economy — with benefits engineered around your objectives, not a logo on a banner.

**Supporting points**
- Audience alignment: agri-focused SMEs, farmers, corporates and institutions — precisely the segments banks, telecoms, insurers and agri corporates are targeting.
- Category exclusivity options [to be confirmed] so your competitors are not beside you.
- Benefits across the full campaign (9+ months of digital visibility), not just two event days.
- Content and speaking integration for thought leadership, subject to programme approval.

**Proof approach:** benefit-by-benefit delivery plan in every proposal; campaign channel plan shared openly; post-event delivery report committed in advance (what was promised vs delivered).

**Objections and responses**

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "No audience numbers to justify spend." | We provide proposed targets with baseline/target/stretch scenarios, monthly registration updates, and benefits weighted toward deliverables we fully control (branding, content, campaign visibility). |
| "Budget cycle already committed." | Packages are modular and can phase across financial years [structure to be confirmed]; smaller entry tiers exist. |
| "How is this different from sponsoring an established Lagos event?" | Lower clutter, higher share of voice, and first-mover association with the South East growth story. |

**Do:** lead with their objectives; commit to delivery reporting. **Don't:** claim reach we cannot evidence; imply other sponsors are confirmed.

### 2.3 Delegate

**Headline proposition:** Two days that pay for themselves — meet suppliers, buyers, financiers and technology you can act on immediately.

**Supporting points:** exhibition floor across the full agri value chain; practical programme sessions [agenda to be confirmed]; finance and investment presence targeted; networking designed for follow-up (structured formats, not just aisles).

**Proof approach:** publish the programme as it is confirmed; profile exhibitor categories (not invented names); venue and logistics facts; transparent "what to expect" content.

**Objections and responses**

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Is it worth travelling for?" | The programme and exhibitor directory will be public before you book; delegation and group options reduce cost [to be confirmed]. |
| "These events are all talk." | The design is transactional: exhibition, demos and structured meetings sit at the centre, with content in support. |
| "Cost of attending." | Ticket tiers including SME/farmer/student rates [pricing to be confirmed]; two days maximises return on one trip. |

**Do:** be concrete about what a visitor can do hour-by-hour. **Don't:** promise specific speakers, deals or attendee numbers before confirmation.

### 2.4 Investor

**Headline proposition:** A concentrated, efficient look at agribusiness deal flow in one of Africa's under-covered regions.

**Supporting points:** startup zone and pitch opportunities [format to be confirmed]; SME and cooperative presence for portfolio scouting; policymaker access for regulatory insight; itinerary efficiency — one trip, whole ecosystem.

**Proof approach:** publish investor programme structure once approved; share anonymised pipeline composition of registered startups/SMEs pre-event; no claims about deal volumes.

**Objections and responses:** *"Deal quality unproven"* → curation criteria for the startup/SME showcase will be published; you judge the filter before you fly. *"I can source remotely"* → ground truth, management quality and regional context do not travel well by pitch deck.

**Do:** emphasise curation and efficiency. **Don't:** forecast deal counts or valuations; never imply named investors are attending.

### 2.5 Startup

**Headline proposition:** The most affordable way to put your product in front of customers, press and investors at the same time.

**Supporting points:** startup package sized for early-stage budgets [pricing to be confirmed]; pitch/demo visibility [to be confirmed]; proximity to corporates, DFIs and investors being targeted for attendance; media presence for launch moments.

**Proof approach:** transparent package contents; publish selection criteria; showcase the campaign's investor/corporate targeting plan rather than promising outcomes.

**Objections and responses:** *"No budget"* → startup tier and shared-stand options [to be confirmed]; cost per meaningful conversation is the metric that matters. *"Will investors really be there?"* → we will publish the investor programme and registered categories; we will not name attendees before confirmation.

**Do:** talk cost-per-conversation and launch-moment value. **Don't:** guarantee funding outcomes or investor meetings.

### 2.6 Government / Institutional

**Headline proposition:** A ready-made platform to advance agricultural transformation, trade and investment objectives — organised by the private sector, open to public partnership.

**Supporting points:** direct engagement with farmers, SMEs and investors in one convening; visibility for programmes and policy initiatives; regional development narrative aligned with state and federal agendas; structured formats for policy dialogue [to be confirmed].

**Proof approach:** formal briefing documents with only verifiable facts; clear articulation of what participation involves; no claims of endorsement until agreements are signed.

**Objections and responses:** *"Protocol and approval timelines"* → we engage 9 months out precisely for this; materials support internal approval processes. *"What exactly is being asked of us?"* → a specific, written participation menu [to be confirmed], from attendance to pavilion to endorsement.

**Do:** be precise, formal and patient; route all contact through human approval. **Don't:** announce any government involvement before written confirmation; never imply endorsement.

### 2.7 International Participant

**Headline proposition:** A structured, low-friction entry point into Nigerian and West African agribusiness — with the introductions arranged before you land.

**Supporting points:** delegation support (visa guidance letters, hotel partners, ground logistics [details to be confirmed]); matchmaking with vetted counterparties [programme to be confirmed]; market-context briefings; English-speaking commercial hub with direct regional access.

**Proof approach:** publish the delegation support pack contents; document logistics facts (venue, travel routes, accommodation options) accurately; use attributed public market data for context.

**Objections and responses:** *"Travel risk and unfamiliarity"* → factual logistics and security briefing materials, hosted-delegation structure, embassy/trade-mission coordination. *"Counterparty trust"* → structured introductions and verification approach for matchmaking participants [criteria to be confirmed].

**Do:** be factual and logistics-forward. **Don't:** overstate ease or gloss over practical questions; no unverifiable market statistics.

### 2.8 Media Partner

**Headline proposition:** Own the story of African agribusiness in 2027 — privileged access to the people, launches and deals of the expo in exchange for reach.

**Supporting points:** accreditation with real access (interviews, briefings, content facilities [to be confirmed]); a steady pipeline of story material across the 9-month campaign; partner inventory (branding, sessions) for formal media partners [terms to be confirmed].

**Proof approach:** deliver a professional press office function from Phase 2; every release fact-checked and human-approved; embargoed access honoured.

**Objections and responses:** *"First edition — is it newsworthy?"* → the regional economic angle and agritech storyline stand on their own; we supply access, not hype. *"What do we get as a partner?"* → written benefit schedule per tier [to be confirmed].

**Do:** serve journalists' needs first. **Don't:** issue releases containing unapproved claims; never inflate significance with invented numbers.

### 2.9 University / Research

**Headline proposition:** Put your research, your institution and your students in front of the industry that should be hiring, funding and applying them.

**Supporting points:** institutional stand rates [to be confirmed]; research showcase and student engagement opportunities [format to be confirmed]; industry linkage for collaboration and commercialisation; visibility with development organisations and government.

**Proof approach:** publish the academic participation menu; document industry categories being targeted; no promises of specific collaborations.

**Objections and responses:** *"No budget line for expos"* → institutional rates and shared pavilion options [to be confirmed]; frame as dissemination and student development spend. *"Industry rarely engages"* → the format engineers engagement: demos, showcases and matchmaking rather than posters in a corridor.

**Do:** speak to dissemination, funding linkage and student outcomes. **Don't:** promise research partnerships or funding.
