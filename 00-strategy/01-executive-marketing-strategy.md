## 2. Event Positioning

**Positioning statement:** The AGIX Agribusiness & Agritech Expo 2027 is South East Nigeria's flagship two-day marketplace where agribusiness buyers, sellers, innovators, financiers and policymakers meet to trade, invest and modernise African agriculture.

Positioning pillars:

1. **The South East gateway.** Enugu is positioned as the commercial hub for agribusiness across Nigeria's South East, connecting the region's producers and SMEs to national and international markets. Most large agri events cluster in Lagos and Abuja; AGIX claims the under-served South East corridor.
2. **Technology-forward.** Not a traditional trade fair — the expo foregrounds agritech: precision agriculture, drones, farm software, cold chain, renewable energy for agriculture, fintech for farmers.
3. **Deal-oriented.** Designed around transactions and partnerships (exhibitor–buyer meetings, investor sessions, government engagement), not passive attendance.
4. **Pan-African and international by design.** Built from day one to attract West African, continental and selected international trade and development audiences.

## 3. Core Value Proposition

**Master proposition:** Two days in Enugu that connect the people who grow, process, finance, regulate and modernise African agriculture — in one venue, with structured opportunities to sell, buy, invest and partner.

- For **exhibitors**: direct access to a concentrated regional buyer audience and a first-mover position in an under-served market.
- For **sponsors**: category visibility with agribusiness decision-makers, SMEs and farming communities in a high-growth region.
- For **delegates**: practical exposure to technology, finance and market opportunities they can act on immediately.
- For **institutions and development organisations**: an efficient platform to reach and support agribusiness SMEs and smallholders at scale.

## 11. Automation-First Operating Model

Designed for **≥90% automation, ≤10% human involvement**.

| Assistant executes autonomously | Human approval required |
|---|---|
| Content drafting and scheduling, email/WhatsApp cadences, lead-list building and enrichment, CRM updates, outbound sequences from approved templates, ad optimisation within approved budgets, reporting dashboards, routine enquiry responses, landing-page updates | Strategy and pricing sign-off, prospectus and public announcements, sensitive statements (government, competitors, claims), credentials and platform access, key meetings and negotiations, signing any agreement, budget approvals, legally required matters (data protection, contracts, permits) |

## 12. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slow exhibitor uptake (new event, no track record) | Medium | High | Early-bird incentives [pending approval], founding-exhibitor framing, strong prospectus, association-channel selling |
| Sponsor cycles longer than campaign window | Medium | High | Open conversations in Phase 2; modular packages; DFI/development budgets as parallel track |
| Date clash with competing events | Medium | Medium | Landscape date-mapping in Phase 1; emphasise differentiated regional audience |
| Approval bottlenecks slow automated engine | Medium | High | Batched weekly approval queue; pre-approved template library; clear decision rights (Section 13) |
| Travel/logistics friction for international audiences | Medium | Medium | Early visa/travel guidance content, embassy engagement, hybrid content options [to be confirmed] |
| Macro/currency volatility affecting budgets | Medium | Medium | Dual-currency pricing option [to be confirmed], conservative baseline targets |
| Security or infrastructure perceptions of the region | Low–Medium | Medium | Factual venue/logistics content, institutional endorsements, testimonials from site visits |
| Registration no-show gap (free tickets) | High | Medium | Confirmation cadences, WhatsApp reminders, over-registration modelling in countdown phase |
